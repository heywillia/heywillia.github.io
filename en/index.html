About

I get into the problem, not the request.

I work in brand and art direction: identity, visual system and the coordination of everything needed for that system to exist in the real world — signage, fleet wraps, interiors, brandbooks, events and websites. Some of these projects were developed at pastafro.la.

Nine years as creative partner of a Toyota group, the identity of a Web3 community in Peru and brands built from a blank page. The projects I care about most start with someone who doubts: convincing is also part of the job.

Now

Designer at Grupo GreenCo: everything that goes out on the websites and sales channels of three brands —the B2B group, the Biotienda ecommerce and the Green Food Makers food line—, plus the key visuals for each campaign and event. Alongside that, I'm building my own PWAs: ideas I had stored away and want to see working. The ones already live.

What isn't here

Nine years with Bhassa, five with Home Market. A good part of that work doesn't fit in a portfolio: meetings, quotes that never happened, decisions that had to be defended. You can't see it, but it's in how I work today.
