01
Problem

A factory with its own product, presenting itself with no presence in the industry. And with nowhere to show it.

The B2B buyer doesn't arrive through an Instagram story. They arrive looking for trust: that the factory exists, that the catalog is clear and that there's someone to talk to. That called for two things —a serious identity and a site to hold it up— and nothing more than that.

The site is deliberately simple. Nothing more was needed: it has to load fast, say what's manufactured and open a conversation.
